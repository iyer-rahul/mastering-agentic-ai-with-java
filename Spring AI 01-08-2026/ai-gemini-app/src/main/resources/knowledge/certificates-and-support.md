# Certificates, Support and Accounts

## Certificates

A certificate is issued when you finish 100% of the videos and submit the final
project. It carries a unique verification id in the format `TLK-<year>-<6 digits>`,
for example TLK-2026-004312, and anyone can verify it at telusko.example/verify.

Certificates are free the first time. A re-issue, for instance if you want your name
spelled differently, costs INR 200 and takes 3 working days.

We do not issue certificates for partially completed courses, and we do not backdate
them.

## Support channels

- Discord: fastest for code doubts, usually answered within a couple of hours by
  mentors or other learners.
- Email support@telusko.example: for billing, access and account problems.
  Response within one working day.
- One-on-one mentorship: INR 800 for a 45 minute slot, bookable from the dashboard.
  Available Monday to Friday, 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM IST.

## Account rules

One account is for one person. Sharing login credentials leads to the account being
locked; unlocking requires an email from the registered address.

You can change your registered email once every 90 days from the dashboard.

## Device limits

You can be logged in on three devices at a time. Video downloads for offline viewing
are allowed on the mobile app only, and downloaded videos expire after 30 days.

## Course updates

When a course is updated, existing learners get the new content free. You will get an
email whenever more than 20% of a course you own has changed.
