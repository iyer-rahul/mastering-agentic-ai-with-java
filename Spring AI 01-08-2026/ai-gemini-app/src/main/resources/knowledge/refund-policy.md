# Telusko Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective from 1 April 2026. Applies to all self-paced and live-batch courses.

## Cooling-off window

Every learner gets a 7-day cooling-off window that starts on the day of payment,
not on the day the batch begins. Inside this window you get a 100% refund with no
questions asked, provided you have watched less than 20% of the course.

If you have watched more than 20% of the content, the refund drops to 50%.

## After 7 days

Between day 8 and day 30 we issue Telusko credit instead of money. Credit is worth
110% of what you paid, never expires, and can be used on any course including live
batches. After day 30 no refund and no credit is issued.

## Live batch cancellations

If Telusko cancels or postpones a live batch, you always get a full cash refund
regardless of how many days have passed, plus a 15% discount coupon for your next
enrollment.

If you personally cannot attend a live batch you have paid for, you may transfer
your seat to the next batch once, free of charge. A second transfer costs INR 300.

## How to request

Email refunds@telusko.example with your order id. Refunds are processed within
5 to 7 working days and are credited back to the original payment method. UPI
refunds usually land the same day; international cards can take up to 14 days.

## Not refundable

Certificate re-issue fees, one-on-one mentorship hours that have already been used,
and any course bought using Telusko credit are not refundable.
